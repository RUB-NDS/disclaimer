Why am I receiving network traffic from this machine?

These connections and HTTP requests are part of Internet-wide research conducted by the Chair for Network and Data Security (NDS) at Ruhr University Bochum, Germany. Our measurements help us understand how Internet services are deployed and study global trends in web and protocol security.

Depending on the study, we make benign connection attempts to public IP addresses and ports or visit publicly accessible websites. For web measurements, we select websites from sources such as the Tranco top sites ranking and the Chrome User Experience Report (CrUX) popularity ranking.

During these scans, we do not attempt to exploit vulnerabilities, guess passwords, or change device configurations. We collect only data that is publicly accessible to anyone visiting a website or connecting to a service at a particular address and port.

Our measurement tools include the ZMap network scanner and the Playwright browser automation framework. The ZMap project provides examples of academic research using its measurement tools.

What do we research?

Our chair's research areas include:

  • Web and browser security: web applications, authentication, single sign-on, and privacy.
  • Protocol security: cryptographic protocols such as Transport Layer Security (TLS) and Secure Shell (SSH), including their implementations and deployment.
  • Email and document security: encryption, digital signatures, and the secure processing of messages and documents.
  • Applied cryptography: the analysis and secure use of cryptographic mechanisms in real-world systems.

Can I request that my website or network be excluded?

To have your website, host, or network excluded from future scans conducted by us, please contact abuse-nds+dfn@lists.ruhr-uni-bochum.de and include the domain names, IP addresses, or network ranges (CIDR blocks) you would like us to exclude.
